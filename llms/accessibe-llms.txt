# accessiBe

accessiBe is a technology company providing a comprehensive suite of AI-powered and expert-led web accessibility solutions. Its mission is to make the internet accessible to people with disabilities, enabling businesses of all sizes to achieve and maintain compliance with standards such as the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) and the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA). The ecosystem includes automated remediation tools, developer platforms, manual expert services, and litigation support to create an inclusive digital experience.

## Products: AI-Powered & Developer Tools

accessWidget: AI-Powered Website Accessibility: https://accessibe.com/accesswidget - An AI-powered, automated solution that provides WCAG 2.1 AA-level remediation for websites. It works by scanning and analyzing a site's structure and elements, then applying fixes for screen reader compatibility and keyboard navigation, typically within 48 hours of installation. The solution includes a customizable accessibility interface that allows end-users to make real-time adjustments to content, coloring, and display options. It also automatically generates an accessibility statement for the website.

accessScan: Free Accessibility Checker: https://accessibe.com/accessscan - A free, instant tool that scans a single website URL to check for accessibility and ADA compliance issues. It provides an actionable, downloadable report, serving as the first step for businesses to understand their compliance status and mitigate legal risk. It is a key tool for lead generation and problem discovery.

accessFlow: Developer-First Accessibility Platform: https://accessibe.com/accessflow - An all-in-one developer platform designed to test, monitor, and improve the accessibility of websites and web applications from the ground up. It integrates directly into the development lifecycle, allowing teams to build accessible code natively and proactively address issues before they are deployed.

Integrations & Platforms: https://accessibe.com/integrations - A central resource detailing seamless integration with popular Content Management Systems (CMS) and website builders. This enables easy, no-code installation of accessiBe's solutions on platforms including WordPress, Shopify, BigCommerce, Wix, Webflow, and Squarespace.
